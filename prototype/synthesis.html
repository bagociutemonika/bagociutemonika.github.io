Synthesis

From raw observations to patterns

Notes from unmoderated sessions with 4 participants, 2 cafe managers and 2 art school students.

P1 · Cafe manager
P2 · Cafe manager
P3 · Art student
P4 · Art student

1 · Value proposition unclear on first look

Both sides hesitated to say what the platform does, before clicking anything

"Is this like Instagram for cafes, or more like a booking site?"P1
Paused on the home screen for a few seconds before choosing a roleP3
"I wasn't sure if I was signing up to sell art or just display it"P4

2 · Uncertainty about commitment and cost

Venue side wanted to know what they were agreeing to before browsing further

"Do I have to pay to list my cafe, or does the artist pay?"P1
Looked for a pricing or terms link that wasn't thereP2
"I'd want to know what happens if the art doesn't sell"P2

3 · Portfolio quality and trust

Venue managers wanted more confidence before approving someone

"Three photos isn't enough for me to judge if their work fits my space"P1
Wanted to see where else the artist had exhibited beforeP2
Uploaded only one photo during testing, unprompted to add moreP4

4 · Navigation habits

How people actually moved through the browse screen

Scrolled through all listed venues rather than using searchP3
Also browsed by scrolling; did not notice the search tab at allP4
Used the tabs confidently when reviewing applicationsP1

5 · The approval flow felt too quick

A one-tap approval raised questions about safeguards

"One tap feels fast, how do I undo this if I change my mind?"P2
Hesitated before pressing approve, double-checked the artist's name firstP1

Five recurring themes emerged across both participant groups. Themes 1 and 3 directly informed the next design iteration; themes 2 and 5 are flagged for a future round.

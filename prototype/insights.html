Findings & recommendations

01

Insight

Both artists and venue managers hesitated on the home screen, unsure whether the platform was a marketplace, a booking tool, or a social feed, before choosing a role.

Recommendation

Lead with one clear sentence stating what the platform does and for whom.

02

Insight

Venue managers wanted to understand cost and commitment early, before browsing artist applications, and couldn't find this information anywhere in the flow.

Recommendation

Add a short "how it works" step for venues, covering cost, commitment, and what happens if a piece doesn't sell, before the applications list.

03

Insight

Venue managers felt a single portfolio image wasn't enough to judge fit, and wanted to see where an artist had exhibited before.

Recommendation

Require a minimum of three portfolio images and add an optional "previously exhibited at" field to the artist profile.

04

Insight

Artists consistently browsed by scrolling rather than using the search tab, which went largely unnoticed during testing.

Recommendation

Keep browsing as the primary pattern rather than pushing users toward search; treat search as a secondary tool for returning users with a specific venue in mind.

05

Insight

The one-tap approval action felt fast enough to raise hesitation. Managers wanted reassurance that a decision could be reversed.

Recommendation

Add a brief confirmation step and a short grace period after approval during which a manager can still undo the decision.

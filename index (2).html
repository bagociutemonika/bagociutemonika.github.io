Project 02 · 2025 – 2026

Detecting item drift in large-scale assessments

A simulation study modelled on PISA-style assessments. I generated data under Rasch and 2PL models, injected differential item functioning, and compared machine learning with logistic regression for detecting it. Related to my MSc thesis at Utrecht University.
